# BakaBoost Design Tokens

BakaBoost uses warm paper and a near-white card surface to make product images feel mounted on a curated shelf rather than placed in a generic commerce grid. Soft charcoal ink keeps long creator posts readable, while vermilion supplies the energy of a printed editorial mark and mineral green adds a collected, maker-led counterpoint. Fraunces gives creator names and headlines a distinctive, slightly literary voice; DM Sans keeps descriptions practical; IBM Plex Mono makes prices and metadata feel catalogued and easy to scan. The radius scale is mixed: mostly sharp-to-soft corners for a zine-like structure, with pills reserved for controls and status. Shadows stay quiet except for the optional ink shadow, which gives selected objects a tactile sticker lift. The recurring signature is a torn-edge price tag, built with a CSS polygon clip-path and a small pseudo-element hole so it can repeat consistently without image assets.
